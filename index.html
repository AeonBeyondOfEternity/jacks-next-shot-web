How It Plays

Fast Multitouch Reflexes & Tactical Builds

Every match blends pure arcade timing with custom loadouts across revolvers, hats, and sheriff outfits.

Dual-Wield Multitouch

Aim Anywhere, Reload 360°

Tap any flying target in the sky to fire immediately from your left or right revolver. When a barrel empties, tap its corner badge to trigger a rapid 360° spin reload while keeping the other barrel ready.

Combo & Double Hits

Climb Up to 6.0x Combo

Build combo multipliers with every streak, hit crossing plates simultaneously for 2x bonus points, and shatter Lucky Horseshoe plates to jump straight to 4.0x and push up to 6.0x.

Almanac & Golden Plates

Unlock 15 Plate Types

Reach score milestones up to 500,000 points to unlock Canyon Breeze, Frost, Ricochet, Frenzy, Electric Chain, Gravitational Black Hole, Fury 2x, Rain, Explosive, Blessing, Starlight, and Supernova plates.

Sheriff Arsenal

Customize Both Hands, Hats & Outfits

Mix dual-hand revolver perks—from Sertão ricochets and laser free shots to Jade Dragon lightning and Sacred Cross healing—with hats and outfits crafted in 16-bit pixel art.
