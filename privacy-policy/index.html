Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Braveris Inc. collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you play Jack's Next Shot (Android package com.braverisinc.jacksnextshot).

Developer: Braveris Inc. Effective Date: November 25, 2026 Support Email: braverisinc@gmail.com

1. Overview & Device Permissions

Jack's Next Shot is designed as an arcade game with optional cloud progression, leaderboards, rewarded video ads, and optional in-app virtual currency purchases. In its Android application manifest, the game requests only standard INTERNET network access. It does not request access to device location, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs, contacts, or external files.

2. Google Play Games Services & Firebase Authentication

When you connect your account, Jack's Next Shot uses Firebase Authentication (@capacitor-firebase/authentication) paired with Google Play Games Services v2 (playgames.google.com):

  • How Play Games Authentication Works: Upon launch or when you tap the cloud account status button or leaderboard icon, the Android client invokes Google Play Games Services to obtain a Play Games server authorization code scoped to the game's OAuth 2.0 Web Client ID (Google Cloud project jacks-next-shot). Firebase Authentication exchanges that authorization code to sign you in natively.
  • Information Processed: Firebase Authentication and Play Games Services provide us with a unique Firebase user identifier (uid), authentication provider identifier (playgames.google.com), your public Google Play Games player display name (displayName), and high scores submitted to Google Play Games Leaderboards.
  • What We Do Not Access: Jack's Next Shot does not request sensitive Google Workspace scopes (such as Gmail, Drive, or Contacts) and does not access your private Google account password.
Anonymous Authentication & Offline Fallback: If Google Play Games sign-in is declined, unavailable on the device, or fails to connect, the app automatically falls back to Firebase Anonymous Authentication (signInAnonymously) or a locally generated device fallback identifier (sheriff_jack_device_fallback_uid) stored on your device so gameplay remains uninterrupted.

3. Local Storage & Cloud Firestore Game Progress

Your progression is stored locally on your device and synchronized to Google Cloud Firestore when authenticated and online:

  • Local Device Storage (localStorage): Stores save data (high score, best combo, settings, selected language [Portuguese, English, or Spanish], audio volume, virtual Braveria Star and Star Fragment balances, unlocked Almanac plates, equipped items, daily rewards streak), offline transaction queues (jns_offline_queue), and offline ad quota state (jns_offline_ads_state).
  • Cloud Firestore Profile (usuarios/{uid}): Stores your displayName, highScore, bestCombo, unlocked Almanac plates (unlockedPlates), equipped loadout, dailyRewards state, virtual currency wallet balance and history (wallet, subcollections inventario, extrato, and ledger), account timestamps (createdAt, firstLoginAt, lastLoginAt, lastSeenAt, loginCount), and offline ad cycle counters.

4. In-App Purchases & Billing Verification

Optional consumable virtual currency packs (Braveria Stars) are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing via @capgo/native-purchases. To prevent duplicate redemptions, tamper fraud, and handle refunds safely, our backend Firebase Cloud Functions (verifyPlayPurchase and syncVoidedPurchases) communicate with the Google Play Developer API and store order verification metadata (orderId, purchaseToken, productId, associated uid, star grant amount, verification mode, and timestamp) in a restricted server-only Firestore collection (processed_orders). Braveris Inc. never receives or stores your credit card number or banking credentials.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Jack's Next Shot integrates the Google Mobile Ads / AdMob SDK (@capacitor-community/admob) strictly for optional Rewarded Video Ads (allowing players to watch an ad to continue a match after Game Over). To load, deliver, prevent fraud on, and measure ads, Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (including Android Advertising ID), IP address, general diagnostic telemetry, and ad interaction data according to Google's privacy terms. When offline, up to 10 preloaded rewarded ad views per 24-hour window are tracked locally and reconciled on the server upon reconnecting.

6. Analytics, Telemetry & App Security Checks

  • No Standalone Analytics SDKs: As verified in the current codebase, Jack's Next Shot does not include Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, or third-party behavioral tracking SDKs.
  • Operational Server Logs: Firebase Cloud Functions log minimal operational events (authenticated uid, transaction status, App Check verification state, and purchase verification errors) inside Google Cloud infrastructure solely for reliability and anti-fraud diagnostics.
  • On-Device Integrity Checks: The Android app checks local installer source, package signing hash, and known tampering package names solely on the client device to protect store purchases and leaderboards.

7. Third-Party Service Providers

The game relies on the following Google platform services, each governed by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy):

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions) — Authentication and cloud save synchronization.
  • Google Play Games Services — Player sign-in and public leaderboards.
  • Google Play Billing — In-app purchase processing and refund status verification.
  • Google AdMob — Rewarded video ad delivery.

8. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your cloud progression while your account remains active so your unlocks and virtual balance persist across installs.

  • Local Data: You can delete all locally cached progress at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the game.
  • Cloud Data & Account Deletion Request: The current version of Jack's Next Shot does not include an automated in-app "Delete Account" button. To request deletion of your Firebase Authentication account and Firestore cloud profile (usuarios/{uid}, inventory, and wallet ledger), email braverisinc@gmail.com from an accessible address and include your Play Games display name (or support details) as described on our Support page. Verified Google Play transaction/order audit records (processed_orders) may be retained as required for tax, accounting, or chargeback compliance.

9. Children's Privacy & Contact

Jack's Next Shot does not knowingly collect personal contact information directly from children under the age of 13 (or the minimum digital consent age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a minor's data was submitted in error, contact us immediately for removal.

For privacy questions, data access, or deletion requests, contact Braveris Inc. at braverisinc@gmail.com or visit our Support page.
