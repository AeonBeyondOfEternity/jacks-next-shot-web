How to Request Account & Cloud Data Deletion

Because the current release does not yet include a one-tap in-app deletion button, you can request full erasure of your Firebase Authentication profile and Cloud Firestore progression at any time:

  • Send an email to braverisinc@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request — Jack's Next Shot".
  • Include your Google Play Games display name (shown inside the game's Settings → Account row) and any helpful details (such as approximate last login date or order ID if applicable) so we can locate and verify the right profile.
  • To remove local saves from your device immediately, clear app storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

Frequently Asked Questions

A red cloud icon indicates the game is currently using an offline or anonymous local session rather than a synced Google Play Games account. Ensure the Google Play Games app is installed and updated, then open Settings → Account and tap the cloud icon to trigger sign-in.

Make sure you are online, then reopen the Shop screen—Jack's Next Shot automatically checks and verifies pending Google Play purchases silently when opening the store or tapping your Cloud Account button. If the issue persists, email braverisinc@gmail.com with your Google Play Order ID (GPA.xxxx-...).

To preserve fairness while offline, preloaded rewarded ads are capped at 10 uses per 24-hour window. Simply reconnect to the internet so AdMob can load fresh rewarded videos and reset your quota.
